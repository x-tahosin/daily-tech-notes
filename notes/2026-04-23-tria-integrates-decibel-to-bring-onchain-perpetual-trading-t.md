# Tria Integrates Decibel to Bring Onchain Perpetual Trading to Its Users

> Tria's integration of Decibel's fully onchain perpetuals exchange offers Web3 developers a streamlined, self-custodial platform for trading, earning, and spending without bridges or custodial handoffs.

## Key facts

- Tria, a self-custodial account platform, has integrated Decibel's fully onchain perpetuals exchange.
- The integration enables users to trade, earn, and spend directly from one self-custodial account.
- This eliminates the need for bridges or custodial handoffs, enhancing security and user experience.
- Decibel's exchange is entirely onchain, offering a decentralized approach to perpetual trading.
- The announcement was made on April 22, 2026, in New York.
